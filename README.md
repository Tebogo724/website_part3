##Organisation Overview:
•	Tebogo Property Enterprise
•	The organisation was The Boshomane Property on February 18, 1999, and the founder was David Boshomane. The Boshomane Property was first a real estate organisation that helped renters find apartments and townhouses in Pretoria. In 2009 it was then handed over to Tebogo Boshomane the cousin of David who then launched it into Tebogo Property Enterprise which then included helping homebuyers find the perfect house nation-wide. The organisation started because David wanted to bring improved property selling services which is accurate and in-depth information on the desired property as well as agents who are available seven days a week to help with the viewing of the property. The organisation was doing well in the first few years until it started losing clients as it was only limited to Pretoria. It was known for being the second-best real estate organisation because of the quality of services it provided. After the rebrand, it was recognized as the nation’s best property organisation with Tebogo receiving the top 30 under 30 awards for the company’s success. In 2019 the organisation has sold over 500000 properties in the first three months of the year. The organisation now has agents across all provinces of the country.
•	Tebogo Property Enterprise’s vision is keeping our buyers happy and satisfied as well as introducing new things in the property market. Our mission is to have well experienced agents and be Africa’s largest and best property organisation. 
•	The target audience are investors who may want to invest in property. Working people who want to purchase a house or rent an apartment. People who want to buy property so rent out to students or adults

##Goals and Objectives:
•	To have a website that gives insightful information to users about the organization like what services or product they render, where it is and where it comes from.
•	To have information that is precise and up to date like showing the business operating hours, the price of the products sold and business contact details that are still used.
•	To ensure that users can move through the website easily and access certain topics quickly.
•	To have user see what the business offers.
•	To have a professional but appealing website layout.


##Current Web Analysis:
•	The website does not have a search bar. A search bar can be included to help the user find the specific information they are looking for even faster, without having to read through everything again
•	The top menu options are clustered and too many. The user would not be able to read the last option which is a drop down of the pages and the content each page has. The website needs a few options on the top menu then at the end a drop-down menu with the rest of the options to each page.
•	The website does not show the hours in which it operates in. It needs to have the working hours stated in the “about us” section so potential clients know when the appropriate time to call is or meet the agents. The website can either say that they operate 24 hours or from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
•	The website does not have an email address in the contact section; there is only an option to fill in a query form where the user enters their email address which is on the links after searching the organization not inside the professional website. An email address should be included in case someone is given a link the leads them directly into the website having another way of distance communication besides a phone call.
•	The website information is clear and understandable each house has all the information required by it.
•	The website has a clear vision and mission statement.

##Proposed Website Features and Functionality:
•	Home – the first page the user is welcomed to the website with designs to make them want to go onto the other pages and read through.
•	About us- this contains the organisations history, vision and mission including the goals and objectives of the organisation. Gives the user insight on who we are, what we stand for and where we want to go
•	Contact- contains the information on communication options that can be used like the company email address, phone numbers of offices in every province and the physical address of these offices. This allows the user to directly speak to the organisation and ask more in depth questions and get clarity on any information they feel they may not fully understand.
•	Rentals- this section has all the apartments and townhouses available for renting including the price, location and updated picture of the property. The client can look through the multiple options and see the agent allocated to that specific area. The top part has a search bar where you can filter the property from the bedroom number to the prices and the areas.
•	Houses- has all the houses for sale. The user can scroll through all the pictures with included prices, details of agent to speak to and area. It also has the filter search where the user does not have to scroll through many pages of houses in other provinces or beyond their budget. 

##Design and User Experience:
•	The website colours will mainly be grey and white with a touch of black.
•	The headings will be in the News Goth MT font, the subheadings will be in the Times New Roman font, and the body will be in the Aptos font. The headings will be in a large font size and bold. The subheadings will be smaller than the headings and underlined. The body will be in the smallest font compared to the size of the subheadings (zoom in option will be there). The important information within the body information like dates and prices will be in bold. There will be enough space between the headings, subheadings and body. The images in the home page will be big then the other pages will have medium sized images.
•	The body texts that will have a lot of information will be split into columns making it less straining to read long ongoing information. Each page after the home page will have a bookmark option where the user can indicate where in the webpage they would like to return to or view again. The pages will have a “back to top” option at the end of every page so they do not have to scroll all the way up again. The top left side will have a search bar and a drop-down list for filtering; the user can either manually type out the properties in a certain place at a specific price or use the drop-down list to select. Images of properties will have a “see 360 views” button to see the house in the neighbourhood, the back, front, side view and pictures of the empty place in every room.

##Technical Requirements:
•	 An SSL certificate helps in making sure that any information shared by the customer will be strictly limited to the company. Personal information like ID numbers and bank statements will only be accessed by the company within its database.
•	 Content Delivery Network allows the information, page, or link to open instantly. The hyperlinks the potential customer clicks on will be processed quickly. 
•	Registered domain name is where the website is located within the internet and the name that can be looked up by users. The registration should be done through a licenced provider. 
•	Web hosting service gives room for your website to run in. It is where the website is, provides data transfer capacity and server power for the website to be efficient. 
•	Firewall wall will protect the website from hackers and people wanting access to private information. 
•	Automated backup system is needed for cases like when an update is made on the website and valuable information is mistakenly removed then the backup copy can be used to spot where it was and fixed the page. Hyper Text Markup Language (HTML) will be used to set out the layout of a web page. The way content shown on the browser is told by the components of HTML. Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) will be used to better the presentation of the HMTL components. CSS will aid in the applying the shades of the website (grey, white and black), which picture a heading will be allocated to and where they will be placed on the web page.
•	Visual studio code is needed to code the website itself HTML and CSS
•	Web browser ensuring that website opens properly without issues on a web browser like Microsoft Edge.
•	Wi-Fi or internet connection for the website to open.
